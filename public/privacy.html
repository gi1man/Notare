Privacy Policy for Notare

Last Updated: August 15, 2026

Welcome to Notare ("we," "our," or "us"). We are committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how your information is collected, used, and protected when you use the Notare application.

1. Local-First Data Storage

Notare is designed as an offline-first application. By default, all of your personal data, including your entries, goals, and categories, is stored locally on your device using encrypted browser storage (IndexedDB). We do not have access to this local data.

2. Optional Cloud Synchronization

If you choose to create an account and enable cloud synchronization, your data will be securely transmitted to and stored on our servers (hosted via Google Firebase).

  • Authentication: We use Firebase Authentication to securely manage your account. If you sign up using an email and password, we store your email address for account recovery and authentication purposes.
  • Data Protection: Your data synced to our servers is securely stored in Cloud Firestore. Strict security rules ensure that only you (the authenticated owner) can access, read, or modify your personal data.
  • Account Deletion: You can delete your account and all associated cloud data at any time from within the app settings.

3. Community Insights (Opt-in Telemetry)

Notare includes an optional "Community Insights" feature that aggregates non-identifiable, high-level usage statistics (e.g., global completion rates for common goal types).

  • This feature is completely opt-in.
  • If enabled, the app only shares anonymized, aggregate metrics. No personal identifiers, entry notes, or specific timestamps are ever shared.

4. Third-Party Services

We use Google Firebase (Authentication, Firestore, and Hosting) to power our cloud infrastructure. You can review Google's privacy policy here: https://policies.google.com/privacy.

5. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy within the app and updating the "Last Updated" date.

6. Contact Us

If you have any questions or concerns about this Privacy Policy, please contact us.
